# Course revision artwork

Edition 5 uses the existing ivory paper, powder-blue, lavender, graphite, and restrained apricot art direction. Fifteen original images were generated with the built-in image tool. Exact prompts and original PNG paths are in [art-assets-v5.json](art-assets-v5.json). Approved earlier artwork remains in place.

All new sources are 1942 × 809 RGBA images with true transparency. Workspace copies are WebP at full size and 800 pixels wide. The only post-generation processing was format conversion and proportional resizing. Each illustration was visually reviewed for text, subject consistency, palette, transparency, and conceptual accuracy. Generated pictures are explicitly conceptual; the numerical plots are computed by the widgets.

## Widget coverage

The five new chapter experiments use their chapter plate. Generalization and sampling use their new plate once, above the chapter. Supporting plates introduce the gradient inspector (`gradient-update`), representation comparison (`representation-choice`), encoder transfer (`transfer-features`), rolling forecast benchmark (`forecast-benchmark`), and retrieval audit (`retrieval-check`). The evaluation experiment retains its evidence plate. Every chapter notebook uses the new `learning-notebook` thumbnail.

The calculator illustration's multiplication was checked: 123 × 45 = 5535. The confidence counters illustrate a frequency interpretation, not a promise about exactly ten outcomes. The AI hierarchy identifies generation as an overlapping task. The representation trays, forecast calendar, and denoising cards are labeled as analogies rather than measurements. Both themes retain transparent edges and legible live text.
